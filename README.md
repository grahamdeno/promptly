# Promptly

Meeting alarms for iPhone. Promptly reads your calendars and rings like an alarm a minute before each meeting,
even in Silent and Focus, with Join right on the alert. The ball on Home grows as the meeting nears and pops
when you join or clear it. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

- SwiftUI, iOS 26, AlarmKit and EventKit
- `xcodegen generate`, then open Promptly.xcodeproj
- Plan and decisions: [docs/plan.md](docs/plan.md)
