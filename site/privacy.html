Privacy

Pop In: Meeting Alarms · Last updated November 11, 2026

Pop In does not collect, store, or share any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers.

Your calendar

With your permission, Pop In reads the calendars on your device to find upcoming events and their video links. It only reads; it never creates, changes, or deletes events. Calendar data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Alarms and notifications

With your permission, Pop In schedules alarms and notifications on your device for your meetings. They are handled by iOS on your device.

Settings

Your choices (sounds, colors, which calendars ring) are saved on your device. Home Screen widgets read a short list of upcoming meeting titles and times from storage shared only with Pop In on the same device.

Network

Pop In does not connect to the internet. When you tap Join, iOS opens the meeting link in the app that handles it, such as Zoom or Teams; that app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Pop In does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
