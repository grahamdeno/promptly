The alarm didn't ring

  • Open Settings > Pop In and make sure Calendars is set to Full Access and Alarms is on.
  • In Pop In's settings, check that the calendar is switched on and set to Smart or Alarm. Smart rings for meetings with a video link; other events get a quiet notification.
  • Open Pop In once after adding a meeting on another device, so it can set the alarm.

Why does the Lock Screen alarm look like Apple's?

On a locked iPhone, iOS draws every alarm screen. Pop In sets the meeting name, the color, and the Join button. When Pop In is open, it shows its own full-screen bubble instead.

Which calendars work?

Any calendar on your device: iCloud, Google, Outlook or Exchange accounts added in Settings > Calendar > Accounts.

Which meeting apps?

Zoom, Microsoft Teams (including government clouds), Google Meet, Webex, GoTo, Amazon Chime, Whereby, and Slack huddles links in the event's URL, location, or notes.
